About Us

Our Story

Coffee Brent began with a mother's simple dream and a deep love for coffee. Elaine Bautista, a devoted mom and coffee enthusiast, always believed that coffee has a special way of bringing people together— through shared stories, laughter, and quiet moments of peace. What started as her personal passion slowly turned into a vision: to create something meaningful that could bring warmth and joy to others, just as her son Brent brought light into her life.
In the bustling streets of Pasig City, Elaine took a brave first step by opening a small coffee cart. With only a few team members and a heart full of determination, she started serving comforting classics— hot coffees, iced brews, and her early signature blends. She poured her heart into every cup, making sure each one carried the same love and sincerity she felt at home. For Elaine, it wasn't just about selling coffee; it was about creating moments— those quiet little pauses in the day where people could breathe, smile, and feel cared for.
As the days passed, more and more people discovered Coffee Brent. It quickly became a familiar stop for many— from students who needed an energy boost before class, to office workers looking for comfort during long hours, to commuters starting their mornings on the go. Each customer became part of the story, and each cup became a connection— a reminder that even in the middle of life's rush, there's always time to slow down and savor something real.
Word spread, and what began as a humble cart grew into something bigger than Elaine had ever imagined. But even as Coffee Brent expanded, its heart stayed the same. Every new blend, every cup served, still carries the same passion and love that inspired it from the very beginning.
Today, Coffee Brent stands as a reflection of Elaine's journey a mother's dream, a symbol of love, and proof that with dedication and heart, even the smallest beginnings can grow into something truly special. Each cup tells a story of warmth, family, and the joy that comes from doing what you love brewed fresh, every single day.
